# Executive Summary

## Decision context

Asteria Consumer Group is a synthetic multi-channel consumer business operating across Digital Commerce, Retail Stores, Subscription Services, and Wholesale. Management requires one connected planning view across actual performance, FY2026 budget, rolling forecast, cash, working capital, workforce, capex, scenarios, and risk.

## FY2026 outlook

- Revenue is forecast at **TRY 1.79B**, **2.6% below budget**.
- EBITDA is forecast at **TRY 164.4M**, **3.5% above budget**.
- EBITDA margin improves to **9.2%**, compared with an **8.6% budget margin**.
- Base-scenario ending cash remains positive at **TRY 245.6M**.
- H1 revenue finished **TRY 13.2M below budget**, while H1 EBITDA finished **TRY 5.1M above budget**.
- Monte Carlo analysis estimates a **47.6% probability** that EBITDA finishes below the original budget.

## Management interpretation

The principal issue is commercial rather than purely cost-based. The forecast protects profitability through controlled operating expenditure, but broad cost cuts could undermine the channels needed to close the revenue gap. Management should preserve digital and subscription growth, correct retail softness, protect gross margin, and strengthen working-capital discipline.

## Recommended actions

1. Assign owners and quantified recovery actions to the revenue gap by business unit.
2. Review driver assumptions monthly rather than reopening the entire annual budget.
3. Use forecast error and bias to challenge business-unit submissions.
4. Gate hiring and capex through revenue capacity, productivity, cash impact, and downside resilience.
5. Link scenario thresholds to predefined commercial, cost, collection, and investment actions.

## Portfolio value

The project demonstrates an end-to-end FP&A workflow: data generation, relational modeling, SQL analytics, financial statements, forecasting, scenario design, risk simulation, Excel planning, Power BI reporting, automated controls, and executive communication.
