# KPI Dictionary

| KPI | Formula | Management use |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | Sum of recognized sales | Scale and growth |
| Gross Profit | Revenue − COGS | Product and channel economics |
| Gross Margin | Gross Profit ÷ Revenue | Pricing and cost-of-sales quality |
| Operating Expense | Payroll, marketing, facilities, technology, and other opex | Cost discipline |
| EBITDA | Gross Profit − Operating Expense | Core operating profitability |
| EBITDA Margin | EBITDA ÷ Revenue | Operating leverage |
| EBIT | EBITDA − Depreciation | Profit after asset consumption |
| Net Income | EBIT − Interest − Tax | Bottom-line performance |
| Revenue Variance | Current revenue − comparator revenue | Plan gap |
| Revenue Variance % | Revenue Variance ÷ comparator revenue | Relative plan gap |
| EBITDA Attainment | EBITDA ÷ Budget EBITDA | Profit target delivery |
| Forecast Accuracy | 1 − WAPE | Forecast reliability |
| Bias | Signed forecast error relative to actuals | Systematic under/over forecasting |
| Ending Cash | Beginning Cash + Net Cash Flow | Liquidity position |
| Net Working Capital | AR + Inventory − AP | Cash tied in operations |
| Cash Conversion Cycle | DSO + DIO − DPO | Working-capital efficiency |
| FTE | Full-time-equivalent employees | Workforce capacity |
| Payroll Cost | Salary cost before benefits | Workforce expense |
| Capex Spend | Capitalized project investment | Long-term resource allocation |
| Remaining NBV | Gross asset value − accumulated depreciation | Capital base |
| Revenue P10/P50/P90 | Monte Carlo percentiles | Range of potential revenue outcomes |
| Probability EBITDA Below Budget | Share of simulations below budget EBITDA | Downside-plan risk |

Power BI implementations are available in `PowerBI/Integrated_FPA_Measures.dax`.
