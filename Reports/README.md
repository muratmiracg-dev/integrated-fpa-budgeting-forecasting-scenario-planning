# Executive Report

`Integrated_FPA_Executive_Report_12_Page_Vector_HD.pdf` contains 12 landscape pages built with vector text, shapes, and charts.

The PDF includes:

- executive overview
- architecture
- historical performance
- H1 actual vs budget
- FY2026 forecast vs budget
- revenue and cost drivers
- rolling forecast and model governance
- cash and working capital
- workforce and capital
- scenario and Monte Carlo risk
- management roadmap

The report was rendered and visually reviewed at 1920×1080.
